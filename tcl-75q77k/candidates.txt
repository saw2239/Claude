# Candidate packages to disable on a TCL 75Q77K (Google TV 12), Apple TV household.
#
# NOT VERIFIED ON A 75Q77K YET. Based on the community list for the TCL 55" 2023 G10
# Google TV (Google TV 12) in https://github.com/Gathin23/tv-vibehack
# (examples/tcl-g10-google-tv/debloat.txt), adjusted for a TV that is mostly used as an
# Apple TV display. Package names can differ by model and firmware: only disable what
# actually exists on your TV, and review each line first.
#
# Format: one package per line, "#" starts a comment. Lines starting with "# opt:" are
# optional: remove the "# opt: " prefix to include them.
#
# Apply:  scripts/disable.sh <ip> candidates.txt
# Undo:   scripts/restore.sh <ip> candidates.txt
#
# Known quirks on the G10 (may apply here too):
# - The red "TV" tile disappears from the Google TV Home row (likely partnercustomizer).
#   TV is still in the Apps row and the Inputs menu.
# - Disabling dreamx leaves the screensaver pointing at it. Fix with:
#   adb shell settings put secure screensaver_components com.android.dreams.basic/.Colors
# - screencap is blank while HDMI or Live TV is on screen. Press Home first.
